## Primary logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production-quality horizontal brand logo with a signature symbol for Upgrade Gate, an engineering tool that tests AI model upgrades and tells a release owner when it is safe to ship.
Primary request: Create one exceptionally well-designed, original logo lockup. Signature mark on the left, exact wordmark "Upgrade Gate" on the right. It should feel like a distinctive independent software brand, precise, confident, memorable, crafted by an excellent identity designer.

Design direction: a compact geometric gate / open portal, with a rising path integrated through its negative space. Let the silhouette subtly suggest U and G without forcing both letters. The rising path is the distinctive detail: an upward turn at a checkpoint, suggesting verified forward progress. Use a few bold, optically balanced shapes, a clear silhouette, deliberate spacing and chamfered or softly squared details. It must remain recognizable at a 24px app-icon size. Avoid a generic shield containing a check, generic standalone upload arrow, infinity knot, padlock, circuit brain, or stock cybersecurity badge. Do not recreate Meta's logo.

Typography: exact text "Upgrade Gate", one line, beautiful custom-feeling geometric humanist sans serif with subtle distinctive letter details; substantial medium/semibold weight, highly readable, careful kerning. Mature developer-tool identity. The signature symbol is about the height of the capital letters plus ascenders, with comfortable breathing room between symbol and wordmark.

Palette: use the existing project's clear cobalt blue #0064E0 for the signature mark and deep charcoal #1C2B33 for the wordmark. Flat solid colors only, no gradients, shading, texture, bevels, glows, or 3D.
Composition: landscape canvas, single horizontal lockup centered, tightly composed with generous clean outer margin; symbol and wordmark together take approximately 80 percent of canvas width. Transparent background with real alpha. No mockup, no paper, no explanatory labels, no tagline, no watermarks, no alternative designs on the same canvas. Deliver the finished logo itself with very crisp vector-like edges.
